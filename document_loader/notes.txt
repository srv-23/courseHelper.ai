NOTES — THE WATER CYCLE

1. Evaporation
   - The Sun heats water.
   - Liquid water changes into water vapor.

2. Condensation
   - Water vapor cools.
   - It forms tiny water droplets that make clouds.

3. Precipitation
   - Water falls from clouds.
   - It can be rain, snow, sleet, or hail.

4. Collection
   - Water collects in rivers, lakes, oceans, and underground.

5. Transpiration
   - Plants release water vapor through their leaves.

KEY POINT:
The water cycle is a continuous process in which water moves
between the Earth's surface and the atmosphere.
